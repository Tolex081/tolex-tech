## Scope

The single-page portfolio (`src/pages/Index.tsx` and its sections), plus the 404 and error screens that share its world. Mode: Experience. Audience: hiring teams skimming candidates. Action: contact (email, WhatsApp, form), open GitHub and résumé.

Constraints from the owner: no gradients; no boxes or card grids, organise with space, type and thin lines; one calm colour theme with one accent, in light and dark; distinctive font pair (not Inter, not Arial); Motion hovers on every link and button; exactly one GSAP scroll animation; keep all content and links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the back sleeve of a quiet record: projects are the tracklist, skills the personnel, contact the credits. It refuses the dev-portfolio default of hero split + icon-tile highlights + card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated sleeve-board grey (#ecedea) with graphite ink; dark mode is the vinyl, near-black graphite with grey-white ink. Hairline rules (1px, ink at low alpha) are the only structure. One cobalt accent, used only on what can be clicked or acted on, and as the single active-edge line. Archivo at expanded width for display and labels, Brygada 1918 for prose and italics. No fills behind content, no radii, no shadows.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns name, role, that he ships things people use, and can reach contact or work. They scan the tracklist of real projects, read honest skill bands, then contact him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rule with name left, section links and light/dark right. Name in wide display type across two-thirds of the width, tagline as the record title beneath in serif italic, a short liner-note paragraph, actions "View work" and "Résumé" as underlined text links with the cobalt accent. Avatar small at right like a sleeve photograph. Side A rule begins at the fold.

FORM: Liner Notes, position 5 of my grounded list (re-roll 1). seed: 9291c78553d4a1b4. Signature interaction: a thin cobalt playhead in the tracklist's number column follows the project in view (the one GSAP scroll animation). Motion grammar: hover steps ink depth grey to full and slides an underline in, via Motion springs; reduced motion holds a still state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
